# Assignment 2 AI Log

## Purpose

This folder is the Assignment 2 AI usage log.
It records how AI assistance was used for Part A APK analysis, Part B bug bounty finding organization, claim validation, wording control, and rubric-driven Q&A rehearsal.

## How This Folder Meets the Spec / Rubric

This folder is designed to satisfy the AI-log requirement by documenting:

- how AI assisted the workflow rather than replacing evidence review;
- how Part A claims were checked against APK code/config evidence;
- how Part B claims were checked against redacted logs, PoC outputs, replay-validation notes, and safe-testing boundaries;
- how overclaims were narrowed before use in report and presentation material;
- how rubric-driven mock Q&A improved the final wording.

## Included Files

- `workflow-summary.md`
  - stage-by-stage summary of where AI was used across Part A and Part B.
- `prompt-response-summaries.md`
  - compact summaries of representative AI interactions and what was accepted, modified, or rejected.
- `validation-record.md`
  - how AI-assisted outputs were checked against evidence and rubric constraints.
- `mock-qa-log.md`
  - rubric-driven rehearsal questions and resulting wording changes.
- `tools-and-docs-references.md`
  - assignment documents, local evidence, external references, and tools consulted.

## Boundary Statement

AI was used to organize evidence, draft bounded wording, prepare presentation scripts, and rehearse Q&A.
It was not used as proof.
Final technical claims were retained only when they matched local evidence, assignment constraints, and safe-testing boundaries.
